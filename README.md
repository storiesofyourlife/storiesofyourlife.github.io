# storiesofyourlife.github.io

An interactive web presentation of Ted Chiang's "Stories of Your Life and Others" - a collection of award-winning science fiction stories that explore the boundaries of consciousness, language, mathematics, and reality itself.

## Features

- Interactive navigation between 8 stories
- Progress tracking as you read
- Responsive design optimized for reading
- Clean, modern interface that focuses on the content

## About the Collection

"Stories of Your Life and Others" contains some of the most celebrated science fiction stories of the past decades, including "Story of Your Life" which was adapted into the Academy Award-nominated film "Arrival." Ted Chiang's work is known for its rigorous scientific thinking combined with profound philosophical exploration.
