Chapter 1: The Beginning

It was a quiet morning in the small town of Willowbrook. The kind of quiet that promised something unusual was about to happen. The sun had just begun to peek over the horizon, casting long shadows across the empty streets.

Sarah Miller stood at her window, coffee mug in hand, watching as the world slowly came to life. There was something different about today, she could feel it in the air—an electric tension that made the hairs on her arms stand on end.

The telegram had arrived last night. After all these years, he was coming back. Thomas Green, the boy who had left Willowbrook ten years ago with nothing but a backpack and dreams too big for their small town, was returning. And Sarah wasn't sure how she felt about it.

1

The memories came flooding back as she sipped her coffee, its warmth a stark contrast to the chill that ran down her spine. The summer nights spent by the old oak tree, whispered promises under starlit skies, and the tearful goodbye at the train station.

"He won't be the same," she muttered to herself, setting down the mug on the windowsill. "Neither am I."

The clock on her wall ticked steadily, each second bringing Thomas closer to Willowbrook, closer to her. Sarah moved away from the window and began preparing for the day ahead. Work wouldn't wait, regardless of who was coming to town.

2

As she gathered her things, the phone rang—shrill and demanding in the quiet house.

"Hello?" she answered, already knowing who it would be.

"He's on the morning train," came her sister's excited voice. "Are you going to meet him at the station?"

Sarah hesitated, her grip on the phone tightening. "I don't think so, Emma. That chapter of my life is closed."

"But Sarah—"

"I have to go. I'm late for work," Sarah interrupted, not wanting to discuss it further.

3

After hanging up, she leaned against the wall and closed her eyes. Despite her words to Emma, she knew that some chapters never truly close—they just wait, patiently, for someone to turn the page again.

Outside, the town was now fully awake. People hurried to their destinations, unaware of the storm brewing within Sarah. To them, it was just another day. To her, it was the day that could change everything.

The train was scheduled to arrive at noon. Sarah had exactly four hours to decide whether she would be there or not.

Four hours to decide if she was ready to face the past.
